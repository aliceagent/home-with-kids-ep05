- **呢 (ne)** — softens and affirms: "I do~"

## Scene 2 — "Handsomer Than F4" (2:30 – 4:30)

> **VISUAL:** Ghibli scene 2 (`scene-02-liumei-f4.png`)

### Dialogue Breakdown

| # | Speaker | 中文 | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 刘梅 | 我觉得吧，他们比 F1、F2、F3 都帅。 | Wǒ juéde ba, tāmen bǐ F1, F2, F3 dōu shuài. | I think they're even handsomer than F1, F2, and F3. |
| 6 | 夏雪 | 那是什么乐队呀？ | Nà shì shénme yuèduì ya? | What band is that? |
| 7 | 刘梅 | 那都是些老乐队。 | Nà dōu shì xiē lǎo yuèduì. | Those are all old bands. |

**🎴 GRAMMAR CARD:** `card-grammar-bi.png`

**Grammar Spotlight — 比 comparison (1 min):**

```
A  比  B  都  + adjective
They  than  F4  even  handsome
```

- **比 (bǐ)** = comparison marker ("than")
- **都 (dōu)** = "even" / "all" — adds emphasis
- **觉得 (juéde)** = to feel/think
- **吧 (ba)** = softens opinion ("I think, well...")
- **The F1/F2/F3 joke** — Liu Mei treats F4 like a numbered series. She says old band guys are handsomer than F1, F2, and F3 — not realizing F4 is the band's name. Xiaoxue's "What band is that?" is the punchline.

**Practice:** Can you say "She is taller than me"?  
→ 她比我高。Tā bǐ wǒ gāo.

**Vocab add:** 乐队 yuèduì (band) · 帅 shuài (handsome/cool)

> **VISUAL:** Continue scene 2 illustration

### Dialogue Breakdown

| # | Speaker | 中文 | Pinyin | English |

| 8 | 夏雪 | 阿姨，您对流行音乐的历史还真有研究。 | Āyí, nín duì liúxíng yīnyuè de lìshǐ hái zhēn yǒu yánjiū. | Auntie, you really know your pop music history! |
| 9 | 刘梅 | 那当然了，阿姨是老追星族。 | Nà dāngrán le, āyí shì lǎo zhuīxīngzú. | Of course! Auntie is an old-school fan. |
| 10 | 夏雪 | 阿姨，那您喜欢哪首歌呀？ | Āyí, nà nín xǐhuān nǎ shǒu gē ya? | Auntie, which song do you like? |
| 11 | 夏雪 | 我喜欢那首《要定你》。 | Wǒ xǐhuān nà shǒu «Yào Dìng Nǐ». | I like that song "Want You for Sure." |
| 12 | 刘梅 | 我也喜欢这首。 | Wǒ yě xǐhuān zhè shǒu. | I like this one too. |

**🎴 VOCAB CARD:** `card-vocab-zhuixingzu.png` — 追星族

**Teaching notes (VO):**
- **有研究 (yǒu yánjiū)** — colloquial praise: "you really know about this"
- **追星族 (zhuīxīngzú)** — literally "star-chasing tribe" = fan/groupie. 追 = chase, 星 = star, 族 = group
- **老 (lǎo)** here means "long-time" / "old-school", not just "old"
- **首 (shǒu)** — measure word for songs: 一首歌 one song, 这首 this song

**Character spotlight — 追 (zhuī):**
- 辶 (walk radical) + 隹 (bird) = to chase, pursue
- Also in: 追赶 (catch up), 追求 (pursue/go after)

**🎴 IDIOM CARDS:** `card-idiom-chouweixiangtou.png` + `card-idiom-yijiaren.png`

**Idiom Deep Dive (2 min):**

### 臭味相投 chòuwèixiāngtóu
| | |
|---|---|
| Literal | Same foul smell, same direction |
| Meaning | Birds of a feather flock together |
| Tone | Often playful or sarcastic — Dad teases them |
| 臭 chòu | stinky |
| 味 wèi | smell/flavor |
| 相投 xiāngtóu | match, agree |

### 不是一家人，不进一家门
| | |
|---|---|
| Literal | If you're not family, you don't enter the same door |
| Meaning | Like attracts like; family shares traits |
| Note | Liu Mei twists the classic saying playfully |

**Repeat:** Say 臭味相投 three times. Match the teasing tone!

> **CLIP:** 1:02 – 1:10

### Dialogue Breakdown

| # | Speaker | 中文 | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | 夏雪 | 阿姨，那您唱两句。 | Āyí, nà nín chàng liǎng jù. | Auntie, sing a couple lines for us! |
| 17 | 刘梅 | 那第一句词是什么来着？ | Nà dì yī jù cí shì shénme láizhe? | What were the first lyrics again? |
| 18 | 刘梅 | 我是骚动，我是爱情的暴风。 | Wǒ shì sāodòng, wǒ shì àiqíng de bàofēng. | I am the commotion, I am the storm of love! |

**Teaching notes (VO):**
- **唱两句 (chàng liǎng jù)** — "sing a couple lines" — very colloquial request
- **来着 (láizhe)** — "as I recall" — casual past tense particle
- Liu Mei gets the lyrics wrong — comedy! She's *pretending* to be a superfan
- This sets up the whole episode: Liu Mei will do anything to bond with Xiaoxue

**Cultural note:** The real F4 song is 《流星雨》. Liu Mei mixing up lyrics is the joke.
